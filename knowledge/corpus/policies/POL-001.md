# Competitive Quotation Policy

## POL-001-C01 Low value purchases

Purchases with an estimated total cost below UGX 500,000 require at least one written quotation from an eligible supplier.

## POL-001-C02 Standard purchases

Purchases from UGX 500,000 up to but not including UGX 5,000,000 require at least three comparable written quotations from eligible suppliers.

## POL-001-C03 Higher value purchases

Purchases of UGX 5,000,000 or more require at least three comparable written quotations and a documented comparison for Procurement Manager review.

## POL-001-C04 Missing quotation handling

When the required number of quotations cannot be obtained, the Procurement Officer must record the reason. The Procurement Manager must approve the exception before the requisition proceeds. Urgency alone does not remove the need for a written justification.
