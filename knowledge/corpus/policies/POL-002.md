# Approved Supplier Policy

## POL-002-C01 Approved Vendor List

Atlantic Hardware normally purchases only from suppliers with active status on the Approved Vendor List. A supplier profile must include its registered name, supplied categories, approval date, and current status.

## POL-002-C02 Blacklist exclusion

A suspended or blacklisted supplier must not receive new business. Suspension takes precedence over any older approved status until management records reinstatement.

## POL-002-C03 Quotation validity

A supplier may quote only for categories recorded in its approved profile. An out-of-category quotation requires Procurement Manager review before it can be considered.

## POL-002-C04 Supplier record discrepancies

When a supplier name, category, or status differs between a quotation and the supplier register, the Procurement Officer must resolve the discrepancy before comparison. Staff must not assume that similarly named businesses are the same supplier.
