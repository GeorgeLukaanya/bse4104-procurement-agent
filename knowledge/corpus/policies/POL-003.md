# Quotation Evaluation Policy

## POL-003-C01 Requisition currency

Atlantic Hardware records purchase requisitions and comparison totals in UGX. A foreign-currency quotation must show its original currency and the approved exchange-rate reference used for the comparison.

## POL-003-C02 Foreign currency

Offers must be compared on the same stocking unit and requested quantity. A carton, coil, reel, tonne, or other bulk unit may be compared only when its conversion factor is stated and matches the requested product specification.

## POL-003-C03 VAT

The comparison must identify whether each price includes VAT. Delivery, freight, handling, and other stated charges must be shown separately so that the total cost to Atlantic Hardware is visible.

## POL-003-C04 Landed cost

The lowest price does not automatically determine the preferred offer. The written comparison must also disclose specification compliance, delivery time, quotation validity, warranty, and material commercial differences for management review.
