# Delegation of Procurement Authority

## POL-004-C01 Procurement Officer

The Procurement Officer may prepare reorder reviews, collect quotations, produce comparisons, and submit purchase requisitions. Preparing a requisition does not authorize a purchase.

## POL-004-C02 Procurement Manager

The Procurement Manager reviews the quotation evidence, policy compliance, commercial comparison, and written justification. Only the Procurement Manager may approve or reject a requisition within this project workflow.

## POL-004-C03 Separation of duties

The person preparing a requisition must not record the manager's approval on the manager's behalf. Rejection reasons and approved exceptions must be documented with the responsible person's identity and date.

## POL-004-C04 Commitment to suppliers

A draft requisition, quotation comparison, or recommendation is not a purchase order and must not be presented to a supplier as a commitment. Supplier notification, purchase-order issuance, and payment occur outside the requisition-preparation process.
