# Quotation Completeness and Validity Policy

## POL-005-C01 Required quotation details

A quotation should identify the supplier, quotation reference, issue date, validity period, items, quantities, units, unit prices, currency, VAT treatment, delivery cost, and expected lead time.

## POL-005-C02 Incomplete quotations

Missing commercial terms must be recorded as missing and referred to the Procurement Officer. Staff must not supply a price, tax treatment, delivery charge, validity date, or lead time that the supplier did not state.

## POL-005-C03 Validity period

A quotation must remain valid on the comparison date. An expired quotation may be retained for the audit record but must not be used as a current offer without a written supplier extension.

## POL-005-C04 Conflicting terms

When a quotation contains conflicting totals, dates, units, or tax statements, the Procurement Officer must seek clarification before using the offer. No term should be selected merely because it produces a lower comparison total.
