# Weekly Reorder Review Procedure

## PRO-001-C01 Prepare the weekly stock review

At the start of each weekly review, the Procurement Officer obtains the latest inventory snapshot for both Atlantic Hardware locations and confirms its review date.

## PRO-001-C02 Identify replenishment needs

The officer reviews items at or below their recorded reorder point. The planned quantity should restore stock toward the recorded target level, subject to unusual demand, pending deliveries, damaged stock, and available storage.

## PRO-001-C03 Collect current offers

The officer groups comparable items, checks the quotation requirement for the estimated value, and assembles current written offers from suppliers appropriate to the product category.

## PRO-001-C04 Prepare the requisition

The officer attaches the stock evidence, quotations, comparison, applicable policy clauses, and written justification to a draft requisition before submitting it to the Procurement Manager.
