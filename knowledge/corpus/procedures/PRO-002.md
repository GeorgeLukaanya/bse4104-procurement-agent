# Quotation Receipt and Filing Procedure

## PRO-002-C01 Record receipt

On receipt, the Procurement Officer records the supplier name, quotation reference, issue date, date received, and related product category. Each quotation is kept in its original received form.

## PRO-002-C02 Check identity and scope

The officer confirms that the quotation identifies the supplier and the goods offered. Items unrelated to the current reorder review remain on file but are excluded from the current comparison.

## PRO-002-C03 Check commercial terms

The officer checks quantities, units, prices, currency, VAT treatment, delivery cost, lead time, and validity. Illegible or ambiguous terms require supplier clarification and must not be guessed.

## PRO-002-C04 Preserve corrections

Any corrected transcription must retain the original quotation reference and an audit note identifying the correction, its reason, the officer, and the date.
