# Quotation Comparison Procedure

## PRO-003-C01 Confirm comparable offers

Confirm that each offer covers the required item and specification, uses a convertible unit of measure, remains valid, and comes from a supplier eligible for the category.

## PRO-003-C02 Prepare the comparison

Record the requested quantity, equivalent stocking unit, unit price, currency, VAT treatment, delivery cost, total evaluated cost, and lead time for every comparable offer.

## PRO-003-C03 Document material differences

Note specification differences, warranties, delivery conditions, validity limits, and missing information. Do not conceal a disadvantage merely because an offer has the lowest stated price.

## PRO-003-C04 Record the recommendation

The Procurement Officer records the preferred eligible offer and explains the evidence supporting it. The comparison and recommendation are submitted to the Procurement Manager for decision.
