# Conflict of Interest Disclosure Procedure

## PRO-004-C01 Duty to disclose

An employee involved in requesting, comparing, recommending, or approving a purchase must disclose any personal, family, or financial interest connected to a supplier or proposed transaction.

## PRO-004-C02 Timing of disclosure

Disclosure must occur as soon as the interest becomes known and before the employee participates further in the affected procurement decision.

## PRO-004-C03 Recusal and reassignment

The affected employee must not evaluate or recommend the supplier. The Procurement Manager assigns another employee and records the reassignment with the requisition evidence.

## PRO-004-C04 Recordkeeping

The disclosure, recusal, reassignment, and final management decision must be retained with the procurement record. Disclosure does not automatically disqualify the supplier; management decides the appropriate control.
