# Cement and Masonry Product Specifications

## SPEC-001-C01 Cement

Cement is stocked by 50 kg bag. Comparisons must use the same strength grade and bag size. Prices per tonne require deterministic conversion to the stocking unit.

## SPEC-001-C02 Binding wire

Binding wire is stocked by kilogram. Coil quotations must include a stated coil weight before unit conversion is possible.

## SPEC-001-C03 Substitution

Different grades or pack sizes are not automatically equivalent. Ambiguous alternatives require Procurement Officer review.
